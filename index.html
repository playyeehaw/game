Saddle Up for the Showdown!

Forget waiting for your turn. In this town, everyone plays at once!

YEEHAW! is a high-octane gauntlet of six unique mini-games designed to test your speed, your bluffs, and your luck. From the lightning-fast Quick Draw to the hilarious risk of Cow Poo, it’s the ultimate tabletop race for family and friends.

The Six-Shooter

6 Unique Mini-Games

01. Quick Draw
⚡
⚡

Goal: Speed

Objective: 5-of-a-Kind

On "ROLL 'EM!", everyone races simultaneously. Roll all 5 dice, lock any that match the target number, and re-roll the rest immediately until all 5 match.

The first to shout "YEEHAW!" wins the point.

02. High Roller
💰
💰

Goal: Strategy

Objective: Highest Sum

You have exactly 3 rolls to get the highest total sum possible. You MUST bank at least one die per roll.

  • Roll 1: Bank 1+ die.
  • Roll 2: Bank 1+ additional die.
  • Roll 3: Final re-roll for whatever is left.
03. Low Roller
📉
📉

Goal: Tension

Objective: Lowest Sum

Follow the same banking rules as High Roller, but aim for the lowest total sum. Avoid high numbers and look for those 1s!

The player with the lowest total wins the round.

04. Cow Poo
💩
💩

Goal: Bluffing

Objective: Catch a Liar

Roll once and hide your dice. Bid on the total quantity of a specific face value on the whole table. Bidding is not turn-based—jump in anytime!

Shout "COW POO!" if you think the current bid is a lie. If they lied, you win the point; if they were honest, the bidder wins.

05. Snake Bite
🐍
🐍

Goal: Survival

Objective: Last Man Standing

The Caller picks a Poison Number. Everyone rolls current dice once per pulse. After each roll, remove any dice showing the poison number.

Survive until you are the only one left with dice to win the round.

06. Bust'em
🃏
🃏

Goal: Push Your Luck

Objective: Closest to 21

Roll dice one at a time and mentally add your total. Stop whenever you want to lock in your score.

DANGER: If you exceed 21, you BUST and score 0. The player closest to 21 without going over wins the final point.
