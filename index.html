The Showdown

How to Play a Round

✋

Grab Your Iron

Each player takes 5 dice from the pile. Get comfortable. Clear your space. The action is about to get frantic.

🗣️

The Call

A designated caller looks everyone in the eye and shouts:

"3, 2, 1... ROLL 'EM!"

🎲⚡

Chaos Unleashed

1. Roll All: Everyone rolls simultaneously. Speed is everything.

2. Lock Target: Identify dice matching the current Round's Target Number. Set them aside.

3. Re-Roll: Grab the remaining non-matching dice and roll again immediately. Don't wait!

🏆

Tie-Breaker: The Shootout

If two or more players are tied for wins after Round 6, they face off in The Shootout. It's a high-stakes duel played in 5 clashes.

  • 1. Both players roll 1 die simultaneously.
  • 2. Highest number wins the roll.
  • 3. Repeat 5 times.
  • 4. Majority wins the Championship.
